Reading the output

The camera view is what a real sensor would deliver: motion blur from integrating true sub-exposures, read noise, and fisheye warping. The depth view is the answer key — computed by intersecting each pixel's ray with the scene analytically, so it carries no rendering error.

Push the angular rate up and watch blur wash out the camera while depth stays perfect. That gap is the thing a real benchmark cannot show you, because a real benchmark has to measure its ground truth too.
